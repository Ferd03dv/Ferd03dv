# 👋 Hi there! I'm **Ferdinando Del Vecchio**

I am a **Software Engineer and AI Engineer at Lampante AI**, where I contribute to development and maintenance of software, including the integration of AI-powered features.

I am currently pursuing a Master's Degree in Computer Engineering at the **Politecnico di Torino**, with a focus on **Machine Learning and Data Analytics**.

My main interests include **Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing, Large Language Models, Agentic AI** and the development of reliable AI-enabled products. I enjoy working across the full development lifecycle: from backend services and APIs to frontend applications, databases and AI integrations.

Most of the repositories available here relate to university work. Some personal and professional projects remain private while under active development.
